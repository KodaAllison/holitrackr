## 1.0.0 (2026-10-01)

Atlas v2. The biggest change HoliTrackr has had, and the first versioned release. Built on the `atlas-v2` trunk across 23 PRs (#21 to #43) and merged to main in #44.

### Features

* **map:** replace Leaflet with a d3-geo canvas map engine, with an Equal Earth flat map
* **map:** spinnable globe as the default desktop map, with drag, inertia, zoom and an idle spin
* **map:** Globe / Flat toggle that unrolls the globe into the flat map
* **map:** startup intro where your countries light up on a spinning globe
* **map:** filters, a zoom stack, "Fit to my countries", a progress chip and a selected country label
* **map:** full screen flat map on mobile with a draggable bottom sheet
* **journal:** place field, and "Hoping to go" dates for bucket list countries
* **journal:** multiple visits per country, stored in a new `country_visits` table
* **sidebar:** country list with Visited / Bucket list tabs, sorting and keyboard navigation
* **sidebar:** detail panel with an autosaving journal and Undo for removals
* **timeline:** scrubbable timeline with a map that fills in trip by trip and Play / Prev / Next
* **milestones:** celebrations at 10, 25, 50, 100 and 150 countries, and for each new continent
* **auth:** animated atlas plate sign-in screen

### Performance

* **map:** country outlines bundled as TopoJSON instead of fetching a 14.6 MB GeoJSON on every load (32 KB motion, 110 KB detail, gzipped)
* JS bundle down from 362 KB to 287 KB after removing Leaflet

### Bug Fixes

* Undo waits for the pending delete, so a slow request can't wipe the restored country
* autosave only shows "Saved" once the request succeeds, and saves are queued in order
* a failed load shows "Couldn't load your atlas." with Retry instead of an empty atlas
* the desktop globe no longer stays blank until the mouse moves
